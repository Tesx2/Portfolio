About Vincent

I am a Kenya-based graphic designer, animator, and motion designer trained in Animation and Graphic Design at the Kenya Institute of Mass Communication. My work sits where campaign design, storytelling, and digital presentation meet.

Clients hire me when they need someone who can understand a brief, shape a clear visual direction, produce polished assets, and prepare them for print, social media, video, or web. I communicate simply, organize files properly, and keep revisions focused on the goal.

StrengthVisual hierarchy, brand presentation, motion timing, and clear campaign messages.
Best fitAgencies, NGOs, SMEs, creators, tourism brands, churches, and marketing teams.
OutputPosters, logos, campaign assets, motion videos, animations, publications, and websites.
ToolsIllustrator, Photoshop, After Effects, Premiere Pro, InDesign, Moho, Affinity, VS Code.
